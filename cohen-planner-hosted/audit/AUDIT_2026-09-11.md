## Findings, ordered for correction

### F1 — P1: incomplete transaction pulls can delete valid history

**Locations:** `monarch-sync.js:129–190`; `monarch-live.js:334–341`.

`pullWindow` returns success after an empty page or page-cap exit even when fewer unique IDs than `totalCount` were received. Both backfill and incremental paths then reconcile deletions and record success. Missing `allTransactions` is also converted into an apparently successful empty result by the transport.

**Reproduction:** two saved transactions; provider reports two total, returns one, then an empty page. Backfill reports no error and one completed month, but the other saved transaction is soft-deleted. This is a demonstrated risk, not a claim it already happened to the user's ledger.

**Fix:** validate response shape/counts, require demonstrably complete windows, detect duplicate/non-advancing pagination, and reconcile deletions only after success. Retain last-good coverage and data on partial/error responses. Test short pages, missing response shape, caps, and retries through the sync operation.

### F2 — P1: housing tools and the main projection use different funding assumptions

**Locations:** `public/model.js:377–410, 419–464, 553–645`; `public/monitors.js:118–134`; `public/index.html:3304–3314`.

Closing-cost and insurance helpers exist, but `run()` deducts only the down payment and its earlier housing expense formula. The main waterfall can sell Stripe independently of the liquidity calendar. The monitoring funding check also uses down payment alone. The housing UI passes an empty or cap-only configuration to `fundingCheck`, losing the plan's actual tender/election settings.

**Reproductions:** adding $250,000 of legal closing costs and specifying $25,000 annual insurance leaves every projection row unchanged. Removing all tender/election availability leaves the same annual projection as two tender windows. The UI partially acknowledges the closing-cost omission, but the headline affordability/advisor calculations still cannot support the requested complete funding decision.

**Fix:** reconcile one definition of cash-to-close and recurring carrying costs through the projection, housing solver, advisor, and monitors. Include sale taxes and usable cash by the actual funding date. Pass the full plan plus explicit exploratory overrides. Calendar assumptions must constrain funding or produce an explicit unfunded result, not merely an independent informational panel.

### F4 — P1: SALT deductions are overstated in lower-income/future years

**Locations:** `public/model.js:45, 73–88`; `public/tax-rules.js:148, 288–292`; `test/model.test.js:47–51`.

The engine deducts the SALT cap instead of eligible taxes paid up to the cap. It also grows the cap at general tax inflation indefinitely. The new rule table actually documents the correct reversion but does not drive the engine; one existing test enshrines the cap-as-deduction behavior.

**Reproduction:** a synthetic $80,000 wage household with no property or charitable deductions has $5,390 modeled state/city taxes but receives a $40,400 deduction. With general inflation frozen, the 2030 cap remains $40,400.

**Primary-source check:** deductions are for eligible taxes paid/accrued, subject to a limit. The statute provides 1% annual cap growth through 2029 and a $10,000 cap from 2030. [26 USC §164(a), (b)(7)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/164).

**Fix:** make the engine consume year-specific rules, use supported eligible tax inputs, and test low-income as well as high-income cases. Apply the same principle to other rule-table warnings; a citation alongside incorrect computation does not validate the computation.

### F5 — P1: tax payment advice combines incompatible totals and can falsely clear penalties

**Locations:** `server.js:1564–1579`; `public/tax-plan.js:105–171`; `public/monitors.js:298–319`.

The server feeds `R[0].tax` (federal + state + city + payroll tax) into a federal safe-harbor calculation, while prior-year liability is requested from Form 1040 and withholding guidance aggregates jurisdictions. It also always uses the first projection year instead of matching the current tax year. Payment amounts lack dates; annual totals alone can produce a no-penalty conclusion. The remaining-installment recommendation ignores projected future withholding despite computing it separately. Its alternative wording asks for only one installment's amount as total additional withholding even when several installments remain.

**Reproduction:** $100,000 projected tax, $50,000 prior-year tax, high prior AGI, zero withholding, and $60,000 estimated payments at year end produces a no-underpayment-charge statement without knowing when any payment was made.

**Primary-source check:** penalties depend on each installment and timing; later payments do not automatically cure earlier underpayments. Federal withholding and eligible federal credits must be distinguished from estimated payments and other taxes. [IRS penalty guidance](https://www.irs.gov/payments/underpayment-of-estimated-tax-by-individuals-penalty); [Form 2210 instructions, latest published edition inspected: 2025](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2210).

**Fix:** separate federal/NY/NYC liabilities and payments, match years, collect dated estimated payments and expected remaining withholding, and implement verified installment rules. Until supported, label as an annual reserve estimate and withhold definitive payment/penalty advice.

### F6 — P1: monitoring loses missing-data flags and compares different wealth definitions

**Locations:** `server.js:763–801, 849–869, 1528–1538`; `public/monitors.js:179–203, 236–255`.

New account overview/history/monitor routes map only `snap.accounts`. The existing bridge carries omitted balances in `missingAccounts`, with `partial:true`; those fields are discarded. A partial snapshot can therefore be saved and monitored as complete. Failed overview loading also summarizes an empty list into zero totals with `complete:true`, although capabilities indicate unavailable. Stale observations are not gated consistently.

Separately, divergence compares observed total net worth (including retirement) with `R[0].nw` (excluding retirement and representing projected year-end). That can flag a large change with no underlying discrepancy.

**Reproductions:** the actual context assembler, given one valid account plus one missing account with `partial:true`, reports complete. A synthetic household with $100,000 non-retirement wealth and $250,000 retirement is flagged as $250,000 above plan even when both sides describe exactly that wealth.

**Fix:** use one normalized account adapter, preserving missing accounts, user-confirmed overrides, freshness, source date, exclusions, and completeness. Compare matching asset scopes and valuation dates. Coinbase's confirmed zero should resolve only that specific missing account, not erase general missing-data evidence.

### F9 — P1: reconciliation drops existing liabilities from the modeled position

**Locations:** `public/accounts.js:173–182`; `public/index.html:2092–2108`; `public/model.js:323, 476`.

The reconciliation sends gross cash/taxable assets to `startingLiquid`. Existing non-mortgage debt is neither mapped to a model debt balance nor netted through an explicit reconciliation policy. The model subsequently carries the assets without those liabilities.

**Reproduction:** $100,000 cash and $25,000 card debt yields observed net worth $75,000, but proposes $100,000 starting liquid and no debt input.

**Fix:** carry liabilities and their servicing explicitly, or use a documented reviewed netting method until that exists. Coordinate with transaction handling to avoid deducting both liability principal and the same future settlement again. Verify wealth equality across the reconciliation boundary.

### F8 — P2: new advisor proposals/decisions do not reach an actionable review flow

**Locations:** `server.js:1715–1750, 1857–1906, 1932–2060`; `public/index.html:5191–5235`.

Normal chat exposes `propose_changes` and `record_decision`, but the SSE event sends only the tool name. The structured result reaches the language model, then disappears; stored chats contain only user/assistant prose. Normal chat renders only a generic tool indicator. The older agentic route has a proposal UI, but does not receive the new tools/grounding/validation. `monarch_GetAccounts` is still the only Monarch advisor tool despite claims about holdings and transaction access.

**Reproduction:** execute the actual stream handler with a deterministic model double proposing a $900,000 home. The tool runs, but neither outgoing events nor saved chat data contains the proposed value.

**Fix:** unify the advisor contract and validated dispatch. Emit/store structured proposals and decisions with stable IDs, baseline version, evidence, explicit acceptance, and reload recovery. Expose verified holdings/spending tools; advertise only tools that are actually callable. Include one browser/server integration test of proposal → review → save → reload.

### F7 — P2: transaction presence is treated as complete history

**Locations:** `public/spending.js:179–203`; `server.js:1543–1557`.

Any historical month containing a row is declared complete, regardless of backfill coverage, partial start dates, or sync failures. Rolling averages take the last N observed months rather than proving consecutive complete calendar months.

**Reproduction:** one transaction each in January, March, and June is accepted as three complete months and produces a three-month spending average.

**Fix:** derive completeness from successful import windows and covered account/date ranges. Fill truly covered zero-spend months, leave missing months unknown, and gate alerts on continuous supported history. Review split-parent/child handling against real provider evidence before claiming spending reconciliation is complete.

### F3 — P2: valid zero values are mishandled

**Locations:** `public/model.js:77–84, 147–148, 323`; `public/spending.js:181–190`.

A 0% mortgage produces zero principal payments and NaN tax from the amortization calculation; all-cash purchases at a nonzero rate remain finite and were checked as a control. An explicitly zero retirement balance becomes $210,000 because of `||` defaulting. Month progress mixes UTC date parsing with local date getters, causing the existing test failure in a negative UTC offset.

**Reproductions:** synthetic 0% loan has `tax=NaN` and payment zero; no retirement assets/contributions/returns produces $210,000 retirement. March 10 date-only input reports March 9 progress locally but passes in UTC.

**Fix:** support straight-line amortization at zero interest, preserve zero with nullish defaults, reject nonfinite outputs at the advisor boundary, and use an explicit consistent calendar/timezone.

## Delivered functionality versus requested scope

These are implementation gaps, not additional reproduced accounting failures:

- Spending totals/classification exist. The current UI explicitly says category budget comparisons, recurring commitments, and overrun flags come next (`index.html:2716`). The requested spending cockpit is not complete.
- Tax facts, decisions, and briefing persistence have routes, but the current frontend has no write calls to those APIs. It reads the latest briefing but does not complete the generation/persist/review workflow. The existing auto-brief streams into chat instead.
- A dedicated job-offer comparison (forfeited awards, replacement equity, benefits, and work-time preferences) was not found. Generic salary overrides are not that workflow.
- Position access uses a direct Monarch token rather than the balances-only NormOS HTTP bridge. Live holdings/transaction availability and complete history were not revalidated in this audit; they must be checked before being promised in the redesign.

## Recommended correction order

1. F1 + F6 + F7: protect and correctly label source data.
2. F2 + F9 + F3: reconcile funding/wealth and valid boundaries.
3. F4 + F5: correct tax calculations and constrain advice to supported conclusions.
4. F8 plus missing budget/briefing review paths: finish the actual interaction contracts.
5. Re-run the suite and a small number of integrated user flows. Then implement the redesign from `REDESIGN_HANDOFF.md`.

Do not rewrite the whole application. Fix each boundary in small commits, replace the diagnostic examples with expected-behavior regression tests, and preserve current user inputs/scenarios. Recheck the remote head before modifying code because this audit is pinned to the commit above.
